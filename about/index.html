Yumeria’s Travel Journal is my little photolog.

Just a small page where I put my photos.

The photos I can come back to whenever I want, long after the trip is over.

A few pictures, a few words, up whenever I felt like it.

No rush, no feed to keep up with.

Just nice places and the days I don’t want to forget.

It doesn’t have everything, just like how it used to be.
